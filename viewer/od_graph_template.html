Circle area follows the people in the area, with the change since 06:00 exaggerated ×3 (choose true scale in the bar below); the dashed ring is the true 06:00 size and the percentage is the real change. Arcs are people leaving in the current 15 minutes. Model output. Counts cover everyone who makes a modelled morning trip (work, school, college, university; every mode), not the whole population. Arrival times use car travel times for everybody, so walkers and bus riders arrive earlier here than in reality.
